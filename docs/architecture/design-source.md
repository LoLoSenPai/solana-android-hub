## Open Orbit redesign (current)

The August 2026 storefront redesign was grounded in user-supplied Solana Mobile Store screenshots, then deliberately differentiated through the `Open Orbit` identity: deep ink surfaces, mint/coral orbital branding, large editorial artwork, and discovery controls that remain usable as the catalogue grows.
